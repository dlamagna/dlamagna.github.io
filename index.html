About

I'm a software engineer and technical product manager with 5 years of experience across industry and research. At S&P Global I started in customer success, setting up 4 institutional clients, then moved into the development team, where I now work as both engineer and product manager on its internal tools. That path has put me in many different teams, from client onboarding to engineering, product and AI. At the Barcelona Neural Networking Centre (UPC) I was lead author of a paper at NAIC 2026 @ ACM SIGCOMM. Since April 2025 I also freelance for early-stage startups and build my own full-stack AI products, working with AI coding agents.
